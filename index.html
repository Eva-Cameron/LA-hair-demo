The salon

A chair on Newgate Street, and the time to get it right.

Laura and her team are colourists first. Every appointment starts with a proper conversation about your hair, what it has been through and where you want it to go, and every colour service starts with a patch test.

Clients come for copper that stays bright, grey that grows out softly, and colour put right after it went wrong somewhere else. Many of them have been sitting in the same chair for years.

Your visit

    How to wash, protect and refresh it at home, and when to come back.
